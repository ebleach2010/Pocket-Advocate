One flat price for 5-6 hours of research and reporting: our full case overview call, my review of every record you share, and a written report within 14 days.

What happens after you book

  • Upload the records you want me to review, including labs, imaging, visit notes, or anything else that helps me understand the case.

This is patient advocacy, not medical care. No diagnosis, no treatment, no prescriptions, and not a substitute for your own care team. If this is an emergency, call 911.
