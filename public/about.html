What I can do for you, no fluff

The mission

I work with patients fighting neurological conditions across the US and Canada. I'm a professional patient advocate and an autoimmune encephalitis survivor — I've been the patient nobody could figure out, so I know how long that takes and what it costs you. I'm on your side of the table. Not the hospital's, not the insurance company's. Yours.

From me

I've spoken to hundreds of patients struggling in the medical system, and I have firsthand experience of how debilitating these diseases are, and how disheartening the process can be. My mission statement is simply this: earlier diagnosis and treatment leads to better outcome. My one job is to get you to the finish line faster, so you suffer less and have a better and more robust recovery.

The purpose

I'm a patient advocate — not a doctor, and that's the point. I don't diagnose or treat. I help you understand what's in front of you: what your labs and imaging actually say in plain English, what questions will get real answers from your care team, and what options are worth raising at your next appointment. Part of my job is locating the best resources for you — including the physicians best suited to your case, chosen with your travel limitations in mind. I serve clients in the US and Canada.

How a case runs

  1. Pick your time.

    Choose a phone or video call and tell me who you are. Two minutes, start to finish.

  2. Read one agreement.

    Plain words on what this is, what it is not, and who sees your data. You and me. That's the list.

  3. Pay $265.

    That covers our call and your written report within 7 days. A follow-up session is $75 if you want one.

What happens after you book

  • Upload anything you want me to see: labs, imaging, records. Only what you choose.
  • We talk. A real conversation about your symptoms, your history, and your dead ends. Recorded, and the recording is yours.
  • Your report lands within 7 days. A written battle plan you can put in front of any specialist, anywhere.
  • Want a second discussion on the same case? A follow-up session is $75, bought from your case page after your report lands and used within a month.
  • Live chat the whole time your case is open. Questions, updates, new documents, anything.

What you get out of it

A second set of eyes that has seen this movie before. Questions your doctor has to answer instead of deflect. A single organized file of your whole story — instead of a shoebox of PDFs. And a written plan you can put in front of any specialist, anywhere, forever.

The honest legal picture

This is advocacy, not medicine — no diagnosis, no treatment, no prescriptions, no doctor-patient relationship, no emergency care (call 911). Your data is encrypted, stored in the US, visible to exactly two people, and deletable on request.
