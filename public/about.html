What I can do for you

The mission

I work with patients facing complicated neurological conditions across the US and Canada. I came to advocacy after living through autoimmune encephalitis myself and learning how hard it is to make a complicated medical story understandable to the people treating you. My role is to stay on your side of the table and help you make the strongest use of the care you already have.

From me

I've spoken to hundreds of patients struggling in the medical system, and I have firsthand experience of how debilitating these diseases are, and how disheartening the process can be. My mission statement is simply this: earlier diagnosis and treatment leads to better outcome. My one job is to get you to the finish line faster, so you suffer less and have a better and more robust recovery.

The purpose

I'm a patient advocate with 5 years' worth of boots on the ground experience — not a doctor. I do not diagnose or treat. I help you make sense of what your records actually say, identify the questions that still need answers, and prepare for the next conversation with your care team. When it is useful, I can also research physicians, programs, and other resources that fit your case and your travel limits. I serve clients in the US and Canada.

How a case runs

  1. Choose a time for a phone or video call and give me the basics about your case. Booking takes about two minutes.

  2. Review one agreement covering the service, privacy, and recording. It is written to be read, not buried in legal language.

  3. Pay $1,200. Your case includes our call, review of the records you share, and a written report within 7 days. A follow-up session is $275 if you need one.

What happens after you book

  • Upload the records you want me to review, including labs, imaging, visit notes, or anything else that helps me understand the case.
  • We go through your history together, including what has been tried, what has helped, what has not, and where you are stuck. The call is recorded and saved in your case file for you.
  • Within 7 days, I send you a written case report that organizes the history, the open questions, and the next steps worth discussing with your care team.
  • If you want to go back over the same case after the report is delivered, you can add a $275 follow-up session from your case page and use it within 30 days.
  • Your case chat opens a week before our call — or right away for a one-time $50 — and from then on you can send questions, updates, or new documents without starting over.

What you get out of it

A second set of eyes on a complicated case, a clear summary of the history, the questions that still need answers, and a plan for what to bring to your next appointment. Instead of carrying the whole story in your head, you leave with one organized file you can use with any specialist.

24/7 Priority Chat

Priority Chat is $50 a month and is separate from case work. You can message me from your phone and see when I am online. I answer as I am available, so response time is not guaranteed. Cancel anytime; your history remains available to you.
