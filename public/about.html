ABOUT

What I can do for you

I work with patients facing complicated neurological conditions across the US and Canada. I came to advocacy after living through autoimmune encephalitis myself and learning how hard it is to make a complicated medical story understandable to the people treating you. My role is to stay on your side of the table and help you make the strongest use of the care you already have.

My story: why I became an advocate

From me

My mission statement is simply this: earlier diagnosis and treatment leads to better outcome. My one job is to get you to the finish line faster, so you suffer less and have a better and more robust recovery.

The purpose

I'm a patient advocate with 5 years' worth of boots on the ground experience, not a doctor. I do not diagnose or treat. I help you make sense of what your records actually say, identify the questions that still need answers, and prepare for the next conversation with your care team. When it is useful, I can also research physicians, programs, and other resources that fit your case and your travel limits.

24/7 Priority Chat

Priority Chat is $50 a month and is separate from case work. You can message me from your phone and see when I am online. You are guaranteed same day turnaround for at least one message. Cancel anytime; your history remains available to you.
