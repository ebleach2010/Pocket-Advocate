Community Assistance Fund

Members of our community experiencing significant illness or disability may apply to become verified participants in our community assistance fund. Verification exists to protect members and donors while requiring as little sensitive information as possible.

Applicants must be members of our Discord community. A Discord username is required. Join the Discord

Apply for Verification

How it works

One shared fund, divided equally among verified recipients.

All net proceeds from the GoFundMe plus any Zazzle creator earnings contributed during the fundraiser will be split equally among every approved participant. The organizer will not receive any portion of the payout.

Verified applicants will receive payouts via Mercury Business Check on January 1st, 2027. Checks take 7 to 10 business days to arrive. If you do not receive your check by January 10, please email office@pocketadvocacy.com.

Verification is simply meant to confirm that you’re a real community member dealing with a significant illness or disability. You can redact unnecessary information. Your diagnosis, legal name and medical documents will not be made public.

You’re encouraged, but not required, to add a photo of yourself for the GoFundMe page, so donors can see some of the people they’re helping. Photos will only be posted with your consent. Not submitting one will not affect approval or your share.

If you add this site to your Home Screen, you will get notifications of all updates, including a running total of your payout.

Transparency

Every Friday, these are posted in the Discord:

  • Number of approved recipients
  • Net GoFundMe proceeds so far
  • Zazzle creator earnings being added
  • Combined total in the fund

Before payout, the Discord usernames of every approved recipient are published in both text and video, along with the final amount raised and the equal payout amount. No medical information or legal names are published.

Support the fund

The fundraiser runs from now through Christmas Eve, December 24, 2026.

You can also support the fund by buying items from our Zazzle store. Any creator earnings from Zazzle sales during the fundraiser go into the same pool. The full purchase price of an item does not go to the fund: Zazzle charges for producing and selling the products, and only the creator royalty is donated. If your goal is simply to maximize how much reaches recipients, donating directly through GoFundMe is better.
