Straight answers

Is this medical advice?

No. I don't diagnose, treat, or prescribe, and I never will. I help you understand what's in front of you and prepare for the people who do treat you. If this is an emergency, call 911.

What happens on the first call?

We talk. You tell me your story, your symptoms, and where you're stuck. I ask questions, take notes, and start building your report. The call is recorded, and the recording is yours to keep.

What if you ask me something I don't want to answer?

Pass. There's a flag in the chat for exactly that. Long hold the message and tap the flag. No questions asked, no judgement; we move forward like it was never said.

What do you need from me?

An email address to sign in, and your name and date of birth so I know who I'm working with. Everything else, like labs and records, you share only if you choose to.

Can I stop?

The chat subscription cancels anytime, and your access runs to the end of the month you paid for. Case fees are non-refundable once your slot is booked, with one exception written into the agreement: if I reschedule you more than once, you're entitled to a full refund on request.

Is my information private?

Yes. Encrypted, stored in the US, and visible to exactly two people: you and me. Every session is private. You can ask me to delete everything at any time.
